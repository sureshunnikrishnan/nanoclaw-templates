# Daily mutual fund statement

The schedule `0 7 * * 1-5` is a weekday 07:00 default. The brief comes as standard: at
onboarding, confirm the time the principal wants it, update the schedule to match, and resume
the task. Follow the `personal-finance` skill's `daily-mf-statement` reference and post the
statement to the wired channel.
